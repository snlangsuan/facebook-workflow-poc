Privacy Policy

Page Inbox · Effective date: 2 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how [Your Company / Developer Name] ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use Page Inbox (the "App"), a tool that helps Facebook Page owners manage their Page inbox and comments, including AI-assisted replies. By using the App, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Account information

When an administrator signs in, we use Firebase Authentication and store the account's email address and a user identifier.

b) Facebook account and Page data

When you connect a Facebook Page, we access and store, via the Facebook Graph API:

  • Your list of managed Pages and basic Page details (Page ID and name);
  • A Page Access Token used to call the Facebook Graph API on your behalf;
  • Messenger conversations and messages sent to your connected Page(s);
  • Comments and posts on your connected Page(s).

c) Generated content

Replies generated by our AI assistant and replies you send manually through the App.

We do not collect payment information, and we do not access your personal Facebook profile content beyond the permissions described below.

2. Facebook Permissions We Use

PermissionWhy we use it
pages_show_listList the Pages you manage so you can connect them
pages_messagingReceive and send Messenger messages for your Page, and resolve the customer's name & photo
pages_read_engagementRead Page posts, comments, and engagement
pages_read_user_contentRead comments and content left on your Page by users (required to manage engagement)
pages_manage_metadataSubscribe your Page to webhook events
pages_manage_engagementReply to, hide, like, and delete comments on your Page's behalf

3. How We Use Information

  • Authenticate Page administrators;
  • Display your Page conversations, posts, and comments inside the App;
  • Receive Facebook webhook events (new messages and comments) in real time;
  • Generate suggested or automated replies using AI (see Section 4);
  • Send replies to Messenger and to comments on your behalf;
  • Operate, secure, debug, and improve the App.

4. AI Processing

To generate replies, the content of incoming messages and comments (and a limited amount of recent conversation history for context) is sent to Google's Gemini AI (via the Google Generative AI / Vertex AI service). This content is processed solely to produce a reply and is not used by us to train any model. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your information. We share data only with service providers that make the App work:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — via the Facebook Graph API and webhooks;
  • Google LLC — Firebase (authentication and database) and Gemini AI.

These providers process data on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own policies. We may also disclose information if required by law.

6. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored in Google Firebase. Access is restricted, and Page Access Tokens are stored for the sole purpose of calling the Facebook Graph API on your behalf. We apply reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your data; however, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain connected Page data, conversations, comments, and tokens for as long as your Page remains connected and the App is in use. When you disconnect a Page or delete your account, the associated tokens and the App's webhook subscription for that Page are removed.

8. Your Rights and Data Deletion

  • Disconnect a Page from within the App, which stops processing and removes its Page Access Token and webhook subscription;
  • Request deletion of your data by contacting us at the address below.

To request deletion of all data associated with your account or Page, email sakkarin.nu@jasmine.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will process verified requests within 30 days. You can also remove the App from your Facebook settings at Settings & Privacy → Settings → Business Integrations.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for business use by Page administrators and is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction).

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above.
